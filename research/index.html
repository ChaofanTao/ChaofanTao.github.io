Research

My research asks how agents can understand their environment, learn from acting in it, and become more capable of autonomous exploration. Further questions follow: can multiple agents work together on complex problems, and can AI help improve models, learning methods and the research process itself?

The work here spans different stages of my research and different collaborations: model compression and efficient learning, multimodal reasoning, robotics, and agent data, training and evaluation. Recent open-source projects at LegoX form one part of that history. These essays connect the findings with open questions about multi-agent systems, AI for AI and recursive self-improvement.

Agentic Intelligence

Learning from interaction and multimodal evidence, verifying outcomes, coordinating multiple agents, and exploring how AI could contribute to its own improvement.

Efficient ML & AI Infrastructure

From compression, vocabulary and distillation to caches, training and serving: how limited resources can support more useful learning and autonomous exploration.
