Chaofan Tao (陶超凡)

I am a researcher and developer at Huawei Foundation Model Lab. I received my PhD from The University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Ngai Wong and Prof. Ping Luo, as a member of the MMLAB@HKU family. I completed my bachelor’s degree at the Yingcai Honors College of the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China. Previously, I interned at Sea AI Lab, Amazon Web Services, Huawei, and SenseTime.

My work focuses on agentic coding and reasoning, long-context understanding, reliable tool use, and efficient machine learning.

Awarded research

My paper Compression of Generative Pre-trained Language Models via Quantization received the ACL 2022 Outstanding Paper Award. The work studies quantization for generative language models and achieves 13–14× compression while retaining near full-precision quality.

LegoX

I am deeply involved in LegoX, an open-source collection advancing frontier agentic intelligence across agentic data pipelines, training frameworks, evaluation benchmarks, and foundation models. Explore the work at legox.net or the LegoX GitHub organization.

Research interests

  • Agentic coding & reasoning: long-context understanding, code reasoning and execution semantics, agentic interaction and tool utilization, reliability and verification, multimodal adaptation.
  • Efficient ML: KV-cache efficiency, RL efficiency, data distillation, pruning, quantization, and distillation.
