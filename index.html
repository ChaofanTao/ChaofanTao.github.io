Background

I received my PhD from The University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Ngai Wong and Prof. Ping Luo, as a member of the MMLAB@HKU family. I completed my bachelor's degree at the Yingcai Honors College of the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China.

Previously, I interned at Sea AI Lab, Amazon Web Services, Huawei, and SenseTime. My work has appeared at ICLR, ICML, NeurIPS, ACL, EMNLP, COLM, and ECCV, as well as related journals.
